# Cross-Reference: Algorithm Codex vs. Transformer Repository

This catalog provides an explicit conceptual and mathematical bridge between the classical, probabilistic, evolutionary, and search foundations in **`Algorithm_Codex/`** and the modern deep learning and generative architectures in **`Transformer/`**.

Every algorithm in this codebase is not an isolated historical artifact, but the mathematical ancestor or inductive precursor of contemporary neural systems.

| **A01: Probability & Estimation Primitives** | `Reports/Deep_Dives/00_The_Complete_Mathematics_of_Neural_Networks.md` | Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) under Gaussian noise directly yields the Mean Squared Error (MSE) loss; MLE under Bernoulli/Categorical distributions directly yields Binary and Categorical Cross-Entropy. | **B04: Naive Bayes** | `Reports/Deep_Dives/29_Tokenization_and_Data_Curation.md` | Naive Bayes assumes conditional independence across input features. | **B07: Linear & Quadratic Discriminant Analysis** | `Reports/Deep_Dives/33_Mechanistic_Interpretability.md` | LDA identifies linear projection axes maximizing between-class variance relative to within-class variance ($S_W^{-1} S_B$). | **B09: Probability Calibration** | `Reports/Deep_Dives/28_The_Emergence_of_Intelligence_and_Evaluations.md` | Platt scaling fits a logistic sigmoid over raw model logits to align confidence with empirical accuracy; temperature scaling in LLM generation ($p_i \propto \exp(z_i / T)$) is a direct univariate variant of Platt calibration preserving logit rank ordering. |
