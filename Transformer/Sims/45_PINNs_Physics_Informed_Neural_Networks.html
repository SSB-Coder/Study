The Continuous PDE Equation

Burgers' equation models shockwave formation, acoustic propagation, and fluid turbulence without discretization meshes:

f(x, t) = ∂u/∂t + u · ∂u/∂x - ν · ∂²u/∂x² = 0

Inverse Physics Discovery

Unlike classical CFD which requires known fluid properties, PINNs treat unknown parameters (ν) as trainable parameters:

∇θ, ν L = ∇ [ Ldata + LBC + LPDE ]

As the flow velocity profile evolves from smooth sinusoid to a steep shockwave, the optimizer simultaneously solves the flow field and discovers the exact kinematic viscosity constant.
