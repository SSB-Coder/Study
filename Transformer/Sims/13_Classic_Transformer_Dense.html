1. Intuition & Architectural Overview

Imagine you are at a crowded cocktail party listening to someone tell a story. * **RNNs (The Old Way):** You listen to one word at a time, trying to keep a running summary in your head. By the time they finish the story, you've forgotten how it started. * **Transformers (The New Way):** Instead of listening sequentially, you receive the entire story printed on a piece of paper. You have the magical ability to read every single word simultaneously.

But how do you know what the story means? You use **Attention**. When you look at the word "bank," you instantly scan the rest of the page. If you see the words "river" and "water," your brain determines that "bank" means the side of a river. If you see "money" and "vault," your brain determines it's a financial institution.

2. Core Architectural Components

  • **RNNs (The Old Way):** You listen to one word at a time, trying to keep a running summary in your head. By the time they finish the story, you've forgotten how it started.
  • **Transformers (The New Way):** Instead of listening sequentially, you receive the entire story printed on a piece of paper. You have the magical ability to read every single word simultaneously.
  • $Q K^T$: Produces an $N \times N$ matrix (where $N$ is sequence length). This is the **Attention Matrix**. Cell $(i, j)$ represents how much word $i$ is paying attention to word $j$.
  • $\text{softmax}$: Turns these raw scores into probabilities (0 to 1).

3. Advantages & Trade-Offs

High representational capacity and parameter efficiency, balanced against inductive bias constraints and hardware memory requirements.

Mathematical Formulation & Equations

The following mathematical equations govern the forward pass, loss calculation, and backward pass dynamics visualised in the animation above:

EQUATION 1
$$ Q = X W_Q, \quad K = X W_K, \quad V = X W_V $$
EQUATION 2
$$ \text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V $$

"""
13. Classic Dense Transformers (Attention is All You Need)
Deep Dive Implementation

This file explicitly demonstrates Scaled Dot-Product Attention.
Instead of using `nn.MultiheadAttention`, we manually compute Q, K, V
and the exact dot-product attention matrix: Softmax((Q * K^T) / sqrt(d)) * V.
"""

import torch
import torch.nn as nn
import torch.nn.functional as F
import math

class ExplicitSelfAttention(nn.Module):
    """The cocktail party listener: looking at every word simultaneously."""
    class="code-kw">def __init__(self, embed_dim):
        super().__init__()
        self.embed_dim = embed_dim
        
        class="code-cmt"># W_Q, W_K, W_V matrices
        self.W_q = nn.Linear(embed_dim, embed_dim, bias=False)
        self.W_k = nn.Linear(embed_dim, embed_dim, bias=False)
        self.W_v = nn.Linear(embed_dim, embed_dim, bias=False)

    class="code-kw">def forward(self, x, mask=None):
        """
        x: Input Sequence [Batch, Sequence_Length, Embed_Dim]
        mask: Optional mask to prevent looking at future words
        """
        class="code-cmt"># 1. Project input into Queries, Keys, and Values
        Q = self.W_q(x) class="code-cmt"># [Batch, Seq_Len, Embed_Dim]
        K = self.W_k(x) class="code-cmt"># [Batch, Seq_Len, Embed_Dim]
        V = self.W_v(x) class="code-cmt"># [Batch, Seq_Len, Embed_Dim]
        
        class="code-cmt"># 2. Scaled Dot-Product
        class="code-cmt"># Q @ K^T. We transpose only the last two dimensions of K.
        class="code-cmt"># This computes the exact relevance score between every pair of words.
        class="code-cmt"># Shape: [Batch, Seq_Len, Seq_Len]
        scores = torch.matmul(Q, K.transpose(-2, -1))
        
        class="code-cmt"># Scale by sqrt(d_k) to prevent exploding gradients in the Softmax
        scores = scores / math.sqrt(self.embed_dim)
        
        class="code-cmt"># Apply mask (e.g., for autoregressive generation)
        if mask is not None:
            class="code-cmt"># Set masked positions to -Infinity so Softmax forces them to 0
            scores = scores.masked_fill(mask == 0, -1e9)
            
        class="code-cmt"># 3. Softmax to get probabilities (attention weights)
        attention_weights = F.softmax(scores, dim=-1)
        
        class="code-cmt"># 4. Multiply probabilities by the Values
        class="code-cmt"># Shape: [Batch, Seq_Len, Seq_Len] @ [Batch, Seq_Len, Embed_Dim] 
        class="code-cmt"># -> [Batch, Seq_Len, Embed_Dim]
        context_vector = torch.matmul(attention_weights, V)
        
        return context_vector, attention_weights


class TransformerBlock(nn.Module):
    """A complete Transformer layer with Residuals and LayerNorm."""
    class="code-kw">def __init__(self, embed_dim, mlp_hidden_dim):
        super().__init__()
        self.attention = ExplicitSelfAttention(embed_dim)
        self.norm1 = nn.LayerNorm(embed_dim)
        
        class="code-cmt"># The Feed-Forward Network
        self.mlp = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(embed_dim, mlp_hidden_dim),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(mlp_hidden_dim, embed_dim)
        )
        self.norm2 = nn.LayerNorm(embed_dim)

    class="code-kw">def forward(self, x):
        class="code-cmt"># 1. Attention with Residual Connection (x + Attention(x))
        class="code-cmt"># Note: Modern architectures do LayerNorm BEFORE attention (Pre-Norm)
        norm_x = self.norm1(x)
        attn_out, _ = self.attention(norm_x)
        x = x + attn_out class="code-cmt"># Residual connection
        
        class="code-cmt"># 2. MLP with Residual Connection
        norm_x2 = self.norm2(x)
        mlp_out = self.mlp(norm_x2)
        x = x + mlp_out class="code-cmt"># Residual connection
        
        return x
