The Fourier Spectral Convolution

Non-local spatial convolutions take O(N²) in physical space. FNO projects to Fourier space via FFT where convolution becomes simple element-wise multiplication with complex weights:

(K v)(x) = F-1( Rθ · F(v) )(x)

By discarding high-frequency noise and retaining only the lower corner modes, the network captures the macroscopic fluid eddies while ignoring discretization artifacts.

Zero-Shot Mesh Invariance

Because the learned weights Rθ exist in the continuous frequency domain, the exact same model weights can be trained on a cheap 32x32 coarse grid and evaluated on a 1024x1024 ultra-dense aerodynamic mesh with zero retraining:

Gθ: A(x, y) → U(x, y) ∀ (x, y) ∈ Ω

This solves the 50-year meshing bottleneck of classical aerodynamic CFD solvers.
