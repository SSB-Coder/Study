1. Intuition & Architectural Overview

Karl Friston (the most cited neuroscientist in history) proposed a completely different theory for biological intelligence: **The Free Energy Principle**.

According to Friston, biological brains do *not* seek rewards. They seek to **Minimize Surprise (Variational Free Energy)**.

2. Core Architectural Components

  • Layer Transformations: Parameterized affine mapping and dimensional reshaping
  • Non-Linear Activation: Enforces non-linear feature separation across manifold
  • Residual Optimization: Direct gradient flow preserving signal fidelity

3. Advantages & Trade-Offs

High representational capacity and parameter efficiency, balanced against inductive bias constraints and hardware memory requirements.

Mathematical Formulation & Equations

        """
        Calculates F = Complexity - Accuracy
        (Simplified for educational demonstration)

        class="code-cmt"># Minimized when the internal belief stays close to a standard normal prior N(0,1)

        Minimize Free Energy by updating the internal BELIEF (Q) to match reality.
